Item-type icons (source/item-icons/*.svg)
==========================================

These SVGs are generic per-type item illustrations (Scale, Hide, Potion),
each shared across many items and colored by rarity tier. They are community-
drawn art hosted on the Monster Hunter Fandom wiki under
Category:5th_Generation_Item_Icons.

License: CC BY-SA (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike).
Source: https://monsterhunter.fandom.com/wiki/Category:5th_Generation_Item_Icons

They are NOT CAPCOM assets; they are fan-created generic art, which is why
both the SVG source and the rendered PNGs are vendored here (unlike CAPCOM
monster icons, which stay gitignored). The _manifest.json records each icon's
source URL and ETag for change tracking.
